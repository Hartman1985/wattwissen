1. Whose data is processed

The app is a personal tool. It only processes data of the operator's own TikTok account: the OAuth access and refresh tokens issued after the operator's authorisation, the account's public username and the list of allowed privacy settings returned by TikTok. The app does not collect, store or analyse personal data of any other TikTok user.

2. Where data is stored

Tokens are stored only in a protected configuration file on the operator's own server. They are not shared with anyone and not used for advertising, profiling or any purpose other than publishing the operator's own videos.

3. Service providers

  • TikTok (publishing of videos, Login Kit, Content Posting API)
  • Instagram / Meta (publishing of the same content)
  • Anthropic (AI text generation; no personal data is sent)
  • Cloudinary (temporary hosting of media files for Instagram)

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept as long as the app is used. The operator can revoke access at any time in the TikTok settings (Security and permissions, Apps and services) and delete the tokens from the server.

5. Your rights

As no data of other persons is processed, there is normally nothing to disclose. For any question about data protection please write to [DEINE E-MAIL-ADRESSE].

Datenschutzerklärung (Kurzfassung)

Die App verarbeitet nur die Zugangsdaten (Tokens) und den öffentlichen Benutzernamen des eigenen TikTok-Kontos des Betreibers. Daten anderer Nutzer werden weder erhoben noch gespeichert. Die Tokens liegen ausschließlich auf dem eigenen Server des Betreibers und werden nur zum Veröffentlichen eigener Videos verwendet.
