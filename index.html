WattWissen

WattWissen publishes short educational videos about electrical engineering (circuits, formulas, history of technology) in German.

This website belongs to a small personal tool that publishes the operator's own videos to the operator's own TikTok and Instagram accounts. The tool is not offered to other users and does not read or collect data of other TikTok users.

What the tool does

  • Creates an educational video with AI assistance (text, diagram and music are generated automatically and checked automatically for factual errors).
  • Uploads the finished video to the operator's own TikTok account through the Content Posting API (Direct Post) after the operator has authorised the app once.
  • Marks the video as AI-generated content.

Kurz auf Deutsch

WattWissen veröffentlicht kurze Lernvideos zur Elektrotechnik. Das Werkzeug dient ausschließlich dazu, eigene Videos des Betreibers auf den eigenen Konten zu veröffentlichen. Es greift nicht auf Daten anderer Nutzer zu.
